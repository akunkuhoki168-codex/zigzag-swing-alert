24/7 Paper Bot

PAPER ONLY Loading state…

Long on a confirmed LL, then exit at +3% take-profit (checked against the candle high; fill at the TP price) or a newly confirmed swing high, whichever comes first. If the long is in profit or flat at that confirming close, either HH or LH exits; if it is in a loss, only an HH exits and an LH is ignored. Short is the opposite: enter on a confirmed HH, take-profit at −3% on the candle low, and exit on a confirmed swing low (LL or HL) when in profit or flat. If the short is in a loss, only an LL exits and an HL is ignored. There is no stop-loss. One position per coin on NEAR, INJ, RENDER, FET and ARB, 15m Binance USD-M candles, 1.5% ZigZag reversal, 1,000 USDT notional, 0.04% fee per side, starting balance 10,000 USDT. This bot runs on a GitHub Actions schedule about every five minutes and does not need this browser tab to stay open. It never places real orders and never asks for an API key.

ZigZag pivots confirm only on bar close, so they do not repaint. The 24/7 paper bot is the official simulated book: it enters a long on a confirmed LL and a short on a confirmed HH, exits on take-profit or the structure rules above, and has no stop-loss. The local sandbox on this page is a separate what-if wallet stored in the browser; it is not the 24/7 bot and it does not run in the background. This page never sends orders and never asks for an API key. Times are shown in Asia/Jakarta (WIB, UTC+7).
